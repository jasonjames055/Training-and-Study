About the score

ISACA converts raw scores to a 200–800 scale and does not publish the percentage needed for 450. This file estimates the score with a stated assumption: 65% correct ≈ 450, interpolated linearly (0% = 200, 100% = 800). Treat it as a guide only. Aim for 75% or more consistently before booking. ISACA exams may also include unscored pretest items, which this file does not simulate.

ISACA mindset: answer as an AI risk practitioner. Risk owners and management decide; the practitioner identifies, assesses, advises and escalates. Choose the business-aligned, risk-based answer, and watch for FIRST, BEST, MOST and PRIMARY.
